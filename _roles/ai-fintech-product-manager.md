## What the work actually looks like

You prioritise features, talk to customers and risk teams, define metrics for both safety and experience, ship in small steps and monitor models after launch. Much of the job is balancing goals that pull in opposite directions.

## Trust is the product

Customers hand you their money and data. Clear explanations, a way to appeal and honest communication about delays or checks build trust that features alone cannot.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for fintech or risk product roles in your market, and weigh equity carefully. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for related management and finance occupations.
