## What the work actually looks like

You inventory systems, determine the organisation's role, classify risk, list obligations, plan fixes with owners and keep documentation current. You are a translator between legal text and daily engineering work.

## Be careful with dates

The Act applies in stages and some timelines have been revised after adoption. Do not rely on a date you remember: check EUR-Lex and the European Commission's pages before you advise.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for AI compliance, AI governance or regulatory affairs roles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for compliance occupations.
