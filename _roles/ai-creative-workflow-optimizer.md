## What the work actually looks like

You observe how work moves, find waiting and rework, redesign steps, add tools, train people and keep measuring.

## Baseline, pilot, compare

Record current time and rework, change one thing with a small team, then compare before rolling out.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for producers and project management specialists.
