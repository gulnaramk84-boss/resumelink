## What the work actually looks like

You review alerts, gather records, interview, document, report and suggest controls.

## A clear case file

Timeline, sources, facts, open questions and the decision with who made it.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for financial examiners and investigators.
