## What the work actually looks like

You map how work is made, test tools at specific steps, set review gates, train people and keep records of rights.

## Review gates

Check brief fit, originality, rights, facts and brand before anything reaches a client or the public.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for art directors and producers.
