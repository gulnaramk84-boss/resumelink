## Checklist

- Reliable internet and a backup, such as a phone hotspot.
- A quiet place for calls.
- Updated device, screen lock and multi-factor sign-in.
- A daily plan and a clear end of day.

## Show it on your resume

```
Worked with a team across [N] time zones, keeping handoffs in written notes and delivering [result].
```

## Daily update

```
Done: ...
Next: ...
Blocked by: ...
```

## Check the conditions

Ask about time zones, equipment, contract type, taxes and how you will be paid. If you are a contractor, compare real take-home pay; [RateKit](https://ratekit.org/?utm_source=resumelink&utm_medium=guide&utm_campaign=job-search), one of our other projects, can help.

## Avoid scams

Be careful if asked to pay a fee, buy equipment from a given seller, receive and forward money, or if there is no real interview.

## Stay connected

Schedule contact with people. Working alone all week wears many people down.
