## What the work actually looks like

You learn the person's goals, try tools together, teach, adjust and check privacy and reliability over time.

## Start with one task

Pick a single daily difficulty, try one tool for two weeks and let the user judge the result.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for occupational therapists and training specialists.
