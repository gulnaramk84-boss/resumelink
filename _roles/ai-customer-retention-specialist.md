## What the work actually looks like

You watch signals, speak with customers, find causes, test help and measure whether people stay because they got value.

## Reasons before models

Tag cancellation reasons consistently first; a model without reasons only tells you where to look.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for customer service and market research roles.
