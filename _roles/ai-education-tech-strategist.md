## What the work actually looks like

You study needs, compare options, plan budgets and rollout, protect data, support staff and measure whether learning or work improved.

## A rubric before demos

Agree on weighted criteria before vendors present, so the best presentation does not decide.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for education administrators and instructional coordinators.
