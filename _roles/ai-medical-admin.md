## What the work actually looks like

You schedule, record, code, communicate, check automated output and escalate.

## Escalation words

List symptoms and phrases that must go to clinical staff immediately, and test that they do.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for medical secretaries and health information technicians.
