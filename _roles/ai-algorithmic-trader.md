## What the work actually looks like

You form a hypothesis, prepare data, test it fairly, review it with risk staff, run it with limits and stop it when it no longer behaves as expected. This guide is about the career, not investment advice.

## Honest backtests

Write the idea down before testing, keep one period untouched and record every attempt.
