## What the work actually looks like

You gather questions from leaders, look at the data you are allowed to use, talk to teams, estimate gaps and propose options: hire, train, redeploy or change the work. Then you follow up on whether the plan helped.

## Fairness and privacy

Analysis about people carries risk. Use the minimum data, explain methods, check outcomes across groups, keep humans in decisions and follow the law where the people are.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for people analytics, workforce planning or talent strategy roles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for human resources and analyst occupations.
