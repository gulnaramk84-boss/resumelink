## What the work actually looks like

You learn the offer, listen to customers, write options, choose and refine the strongest, and test. AI shortens the first part of that loop. The value you add is knowing which idea is true, relevant and persuasive for this audience.

## Writing with AI without sounding like everyone else

Feed the tool concrete material: customer phrases, product facts, past winning copy, a short voice guide. Ask for many different angles, not one perfect text. Then rewrite for rhythm and specificity, remove clichés and check every claim.

## How to benchmark pay

Pay varies widely by country, niche and whether you are employed or freelance. Compare five current postings for titles such as copywriter, content writer or conversion copywriter in your market, and for freelance work calculate your rate from costs, taxes and target income before quoting. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for related writing occupations.
