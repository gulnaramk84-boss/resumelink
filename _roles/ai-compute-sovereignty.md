## What the work actually looks like

You list workloads and data, check legal limits, compare hosting choices, negotiate contracts and plan what to do if a supplier or rule changes.

## A comparison that leaders can use

For each option show control, legal exposure, cost over several years, skills needed, energy and how hard it is to leave.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for computer and information systems managers.
