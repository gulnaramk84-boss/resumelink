## What the work actually looks like

You assess evidence, validate locally, train users, monitor, review errors and report.

## Before go-live

Intended use, excluded use, local results, who decides, how errors are reported and how to switch off.

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for medical and health services managers.
