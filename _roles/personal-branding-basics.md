## One sentence

```
I help [who] with [problem] by [how].
```

If you cannot fill it in, start by listing problems people already ask you to solve.

## Show proof

- A short case: problem, action, result.
- A portfolio, article or talk.
- A testimonial used with permission.

## Choose one place

Pick one platform where your audience already is. Regular and small beats rare and grand.

## A simple monthly plan

1. One example of your work.
2. One lesson from a mistake.
3. One helpful resource with your comment.
4. One conversation with someone in your field.

## Be honest

Do not invent clients, numbers or titles. Respect confidentiality.
