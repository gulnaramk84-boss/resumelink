## What the work actually looks like

You measure losses, lead problem solving, test changes and standardise what works.

## Loss tree

Split lost time into stops, slow running and defects, then work on the largest.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for industrial engineers and production managers.
