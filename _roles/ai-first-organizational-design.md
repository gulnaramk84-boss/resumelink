## What the work actually looks like

You study how work happens, design options, involve people, pilot and adjust.

## Task by task

For each task decide who does it, who checks it and how a person learns to do it.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for management analysts and HR managers.
