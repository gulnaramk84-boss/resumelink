## What the work actually looks like

You gather, verify, choose, add context, arrange and maintain, so that readers can trust what they get.

## Criteria in five lines

Who is it for? What problem does it solve? Is the source reliable? Is it still current? What would I cut if I could keep only three?

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for editors, librarians and curators.
