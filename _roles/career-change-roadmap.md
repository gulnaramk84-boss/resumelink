## A simple way to think about a change

You are not starting over. You carry skills, results and judgment into a new setting. Your job is to translate them into the language of the new field and prove you can do the work there.

## The three-step method

1. **Map:** list what you can do and what the target role needs. Mark overlap and real gaps.
2. **Prove:** close the biggest gap with one small, finished project or volunteer task.
3. **Tell the story:** rewrite your resume and your 30-second explanation so that the move looks logical.

## Money and time

Estimate your minimum acceptable income and the number of months you can search. A bridge role, part-time start or freelance work can make the transition safer. Compare offers by take-home pay and benefits, not only the headline figure.
