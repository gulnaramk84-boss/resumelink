## What the work actually looks like

Clients bring practical questions: can we use this tool with customer data, who owns what it produces, what must we tell users, what happens if it is wrong. You research, assess the risk, draft clauses and policies, and explain the options in plain language.

## What to say on a resume

Show matters and outcomes without breaking confidentiality: types of agreements, number of reviews, industries and jurisdictions, and the processes you built. Be precise about your qualification and where you are admitted.

## How to benchmark pay

Legal pay depends on jurisdiction, seniority and employer type. Compare current postings for technology, privacy and product counsel in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) entry for lawyers.
