## What the work actually looks like

You instrument equipment, analyse signals, investigate alerts, plan work and learn from results.

## Close the loop

After each repair record whether the alert was right and what was found.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for industrial and mechanical engineers.
