## What the work actually looks like

You plan shifts, coach, solve exceptions, watch safety and improve layout and process.

## Order of priorities

Safety, then accuracy, then speed. Say it and act on it.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for transportation, storage and distribution managers.
