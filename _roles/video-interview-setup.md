## The day before

- Test the link and app and update early.
- Check camera, microphone and internet with a friend.
- Charge your device and have a backup.

## The frame

- Camera at eye level.
- Light in front of you, not behind.
- A calm background.

## During the call

- Look at the camera for key points.
- Pause before answering to allow for delay.
- Keep short notes, but do not read them out.
- Turn notifications off.

## If the connection drops

```
Sorry, my connection dropped. I can rejoin on the same link or continue by phone at [number].
```

## Recorded interviews

Check the time limit and number of attempts. Start with your answer, then give an example. You may ask how the recording will be used and stored.
