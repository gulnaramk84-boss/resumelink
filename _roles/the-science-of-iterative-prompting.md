## What the work actually looks like

You define success, test, read failures, revise, retest and document.

## The loop

Criteria, test set, run, group failures, one change, rerun, record.

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for software developers and quality assurance occupations.
