## Why organisation matters

A job search involves dozens of small tasks: research, resume versions, messages, interviews, follow-ups. Without a system it is easy to lose track and lose confidence. A simple tracker and a weekly routine turn a stressful search into a manageable project.

## The weekly rhythm

Pick a fixed time for research, a fixed time for applications, a fixed time for networking and a fixed time for review. Count what you do and what happens. Small changes to your resume and outreach, tested week after week, often make the difference.

## Money and offers

Research typical pay for your target role in your market from several sources and know your minimum acceptable number before you talk to a recruiter. When you receive an offer, compare take-home pay and benefits, not only the headline salary.
