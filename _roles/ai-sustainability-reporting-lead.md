## What the work actually looks like

You gather data from many teams and suppliers, check it, calculate indicators using recognised methods, write the report and prepare evidence for reviewers. AI helps with extraction and drafting, but you own accuracy.

## From data to claim

Define the boundary, collect activity data, apply a documented method, record assumptions, review, then publish. If you cannot show the path, do not publish the number.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for sustainability reporting, ESG analyst or ESG manager roles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for environmental and accounting occupations.
