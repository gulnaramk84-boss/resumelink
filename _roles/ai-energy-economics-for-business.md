## What the work actually looks like

You collect usage and price data, find the main cost drivers, model options and explain which action is worth taking.

## A baseline before savings

Record normal consumption and price first, adjusting for weather and activity, so a saving can be proved later.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for financial analysts and related occupations.
