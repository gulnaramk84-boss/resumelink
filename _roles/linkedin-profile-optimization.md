## Headline

```
[Role] | [two or three key skills] | [industry or who you help]
```

## About

Three to five short paragraphs: what you do, two results, what you are looking for and how to contact you. Write in the first person.

## Experience and skills

- Two to four points per job, with results.
- Keep skills you actually use.
- Add work samples you are allowed to share.

## Photo and settings

A clear photo with a plain background. Check what strangers can see. Private job-search signals reduce visibility but cannot guarantee your employer will not notice.

## Fifteen minutes a week

1. One useful comment in your field.
2. Two connection requests with a short reason.
3. One line of your profile updated.

## Connection note

```
Hello [name], I work on [topic]. I enjoyed your [post or project] about [detail] and would like to connect.
```

## Stay safe

Be wary of offers that ask for money, bank details or moving to another app at once.
