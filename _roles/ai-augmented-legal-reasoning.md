## What the work actually looks like

You define the issue, research, draft, ask the tool to attack your draft, verify everything and decide what goes to the client or court.

## Stress testing an argument

Ask for the strongest opposing case, the facts that would change the result and the assumptions you have not proved. Then research each one yourself.
