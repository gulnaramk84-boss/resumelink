## What the work actually looks like

You map use, draft rules, advise on procurement, consult, train and monitor.

## Citizen safeguards

Tell people when automation affects them, explain the outcome and give them a human to appeal to.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for political scientists and public administrators.
