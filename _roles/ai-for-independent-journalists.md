## What the work actually looks like

You find stories, interview, verify, write, publish and keep the business side running.

## A verification routine

Origin, date, place, second source and right of reply before publishing.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for news analysts, reporters and journalists.
