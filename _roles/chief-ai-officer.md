## What the work actually looks like

You sit between the board, the business units and the technical teams. You decide where AI is worth the investment, make sure it is used lawfully and safely, and explain progress and problems in business language. Much of the work is persuasion, prioritisation and saying no.

## What boards want to hear

Three things: what value AI will create and how it will be measured, what could go wrong and how it is controlled, and what it will cost in money and people. A short, honest quarterly report covering these three builds more trust than a long list of projects.

## How to benchmark pay

Executive pay depends on country, company size, sector and equity, so published averages are unreliable. Compare current postings and, where available, public company filings for similar roles (Chief Data Officer, Chief Technology Officer, Head of AI) in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for top executive occupations.
