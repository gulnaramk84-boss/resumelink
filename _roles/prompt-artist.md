## What the work actually looks like

A Prompt Artist works at the boundary between human intent and model behaviour. A typical day: clarify what a team really needs, draft instructions and examples, run them over a set of test inputs, read the failures carefully, and adjust. You document what works so others can reuse it.

It is a craft that rewards curiosity and patience. The people who do it well are the ones who read hundreds of outputs and notice patterns.

## What good evidence looks like

Hiring managers trust results over adjectives. Keep a small portfolio of **one task, one test set, one rubric, three prompt versions with scores**. Even a spreadsheet works. It shows you can improve a system in a measurable way, not just produce one nice answer.

## How to benchmark pay

Because this skill often sits inside broader jobs, search several titles (AI content specialist, conversation designer, LLM evaluator, AI operations) and compare ranges in current postings in your market. For the United States, the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) covers related occupations such as technical writers and market research analysts.
