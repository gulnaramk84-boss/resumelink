## What the work actually looks like

You set goals and budget, book suppliers, manage registrations, run the day and review results.

## The run-of-show

A single timed list with owner and phone number for every step prevents most confusion.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for meeting, convention and event planners.
