## What the work actually looks like

You visit or call a small firm, learn how they work, find where customer data lives, see which AI and software tools they use and fix the biggest risks first. Then you leave behind short rules and a staff who understands them.

## A fixed first package

A small, clearly priced first engagement is easier to buy: data map, tool review, top-three risks with fixes, one-page AI use policy, 30-minute training. Add a yearly check-up.

## How to benchmark pay

Consulting income varies by market and client mix. Compare current postings for privacy analyst, IT consultant or security consultant, and for freelance work calculate your day rate from your costs and target income. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for related security and consulting occupations.
