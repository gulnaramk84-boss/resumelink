## What the work actually looks like

You set the idea, brief the team, review work, choose, sell it to clients and answer for what goes out.

## A review that helps

Say what the work must make people feel or do, then judge each option against that, not against how impressive the tool looks.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for art directors.
