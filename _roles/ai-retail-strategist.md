## What the work actually looks like

You analyse performance, choose priorities, plan tests, align teams and report results.

## Value and effort

For each idea estimate yearly value, cost, data readiness and risk before ranking.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for management analysts and marketing managers.
