## What the work actually looks like

You define the framework, oversee validation, set limits, monitor and report to committees.

## Three questions per model

What decision does it drive, how would we know it is wrong, and who can switch it off?

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for financial risk specialists.
