## What the work actually looks like

You keep data, engineering, product, legal and business people moving together. You plan experiments, track dependencies, raise risks early and make decisions visible.

## A simple structure

**Discover** (problem and data), **prove** (can quality be reached), **build**, **release** (with monitoring), **improve**. Put a decision gate between stages.
