## Research first

Current adverts with similar duties, people in the field and official statistics for your country. Do not rely on one number from the internet.

## Know three figures

Your minimum, a realistic target and a top figure you can justify.

## Phrases

```
Based on the duties and my experience, I am looking at [low] to [high]. What range is planned for this role?
```

```
I understand. Could we discuss other parts, such as a bonus, training, hours or a review date?
```

## Avoid

- Going below your minimum out of nerves.
- Inventing another offer.
- Accepting immediately: asking for a day is normal.

## Get it in writing

Pay, bonus rules, probation, hours and review date.

## If you freelance

Include taxes, holidays and unpaid hours in your rate. [RateKit](https://ratekit.org/?utm_source=resumelink&utm_medium=guide&utm_campaign=job-search), one of our other projects, has free calculators.

## Useful resources

- [US Bureau of Labor Statistics: Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/): official pay data for the United States
