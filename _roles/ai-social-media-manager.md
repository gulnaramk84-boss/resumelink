## What the work actually looks like

You plan what to say, create or edit it, publish at the right time, respond to people and study the results. AI helps with ideas, drafts, repurposing and reporting, but you are responsible for what goes out under the brand's name.

## A reliable AI-assisted workflow

**Brief** (goal, audience, key message), **draft** (AI helps), **edit** (voice, facts, legal), **approve** (the right person), **publish and monitor**, **review results** weekly. Record the steps so someone else can follow them.

## How to benchmark pay

Pay varies widely by country, platform focus and whether you are employed or freelance. Compare five current postings for titles such as social media manager, community manager or content marketer in your market. For freelance work, work out your rate from costs, taxes and target income before quoting. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for related advertising and marketing occupations.
