## What the work actually looks like

You collect data, model cash flows, visit or research sites, check documents and recommend: buy, hold, sell or walk away.

## An investment memo

Property, assumptions, base and stress cases, risks, recommendation and open questions.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for real estate analyst roles. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for financial analysts.
