## What the work actually looks like

You welcome people, start conversations, run events, moderate, listen for needs and report back to the organisation. AI can summarise and sort, but you build trust.

## Healthy communities

Clear purpose, kind rules, quick responses and chances for members to contribute.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for community manager or engagement roles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for public relations and communications occupations.
