## What the work actually looks like

You learn needs, research, verify, book, brief the traveller and support during the trip.

## Verify before sending

Dates, names, times, transfers, entry rules, cancellation terms and contacts.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for travel agents.
