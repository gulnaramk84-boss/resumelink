## What the work actually looks like

You gather data, model, test, build scenarios and explain what could change the picture.

## Ranges, not points

Give a central case, a low and a high case and the condition behind each.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for economists and financial analysts.
