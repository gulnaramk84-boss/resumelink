## What the work actually looks like

You watch signals, investigate cases, improve controls and coordinate with support, product and law enforcement where needed.

## Loss and friction

Every control stops some fraud and some good customers. Measure both and decide openly.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for fraud or financial crime roles. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for financial examiners and related occupations.
