## What the work actually looks like

You listen, find sources of overload, agree on changes with leaders, train and check whether people actually feel the difference.

## Team agreements

Agree on response times, quiet hours, when AI output must be checked and how to say the load is too high.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for health education and human resources specialists.
