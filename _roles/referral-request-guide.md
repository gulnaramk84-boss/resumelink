## Who to ask

Someone who knows your work, or a contact you have had a real exchange with.

## Before asking

Read the advert, check you meet the main requirements and prepare your resume.

## Message

```
Hello [name],

I saw that [company] is hiring a [role]. My experience in [area] fits [requirement]; for example, [result].

Would you be comfortable referring me? If not, no problem at all. I have attached my resume and the link: [link].

Thank you,
[name]
```

## Make it easy

Send a short summary they can paste:

```
[Name] is a [role] who [strength and result]. I know them from [context].
```

## If you do not know them well

Ask for information first, not a referral.

## Afterwards

Thank them and tell them the outcome, whatever it is.
