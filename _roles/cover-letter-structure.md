## When to send one

Send it when there is a field for it, when you are changing direction, or when the advert asks questions. Around 120 to 200 words is usually enough.

## The four parts

1. The role and where you saw it.
2. One or two results that match the job's tasks.
3. Why this employer: a specific fact, not praise.
4. The next step and how to reach you.

## Template

```
Dear [name],

I am applying for the [role] position I saw on [source].

In my current work I [action] and [result]. This is close to your need for [task from the advert].

I am interested in [company] because [specific fact about their product, project or customers].

I would welcome a short conversation. You can reach me at [phone] or [email].

Kind regards,
[full name]
```

## If you have no experience yet

Use a course project, internship, volunteering or family business work: what the task was, what you did and what you learned.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Repeating the whole resume.
- Sending one letter everywhere.
- Claims with no example, such as "hard-working team player".
- Leaving another company's name from an earlier application.
