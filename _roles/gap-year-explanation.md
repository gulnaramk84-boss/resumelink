## The rule

Say one or two sentences about the gap, then move to the work. You do not owe personal or medical details.

## On the resume

```
[years]: Career break for family reasons. During this time: [course, project, part-time or volunteer work].
```

## In the interview

```
I took a break for personal reasons, which are now resolved. I kept my skills current by [example]. I am ready to work and I am interested in your [specific task].
```

## If the gap was job searching

Say what you did: learned a tool, helped a business, built a project.

## Refresh before applying

- A short course on current tools.
- One small project you can show.
- A recent reference, even from volunteering.

## Avoid

- Stretching old job dates to hide the gap. It is easy to check.
- Long apologies.
- Sounding as if you must defend your life.
