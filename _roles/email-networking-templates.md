## What makes a good message

Short, specific, easy to answer and respectful of a "no". Say how you found the person and ask for one small thing.

## First contact

```
Subject: Question about your work in [field]

Hello [name],

I read your [post, talk or project] about [topic], and [one specific thing] stayed with me.

I am exploring [direction]. Could I ask you two short questions by email, or is there someone better to ask?

Thank you,
[name]
```

## Follow-up after a week or so

```
Hello [name], a quick follow-up on my message below. No problem if now is not a good time.
```

One follow-up is usually enough.

## Reconnecting

```
Hello [name], we worked together on [project] in [year]. I saw [news] and wanted to say congratulations. I am now [what you do]. How are things with you?
```

## After they help

Thank them, and later tell them what came of their advice.

## Avoid

- Asking for a job in the first line.
- Long life stories.
- Attachments nobody asked for.
- Writing to private addresses found without permission.
