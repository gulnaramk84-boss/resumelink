## What the work actually looks like

You gather needs, plan projects, manage vendors, protect data, report and listen.

## Sunset rules

Decide when data is deleted and what happens to equipment if the project ends.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for urban planners and project management specialists.
