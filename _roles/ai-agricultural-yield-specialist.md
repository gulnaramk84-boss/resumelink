## What the work actually looks like

You collect field and satellite data, find patterns, check them on the ground and turn them into a few clear actions for the farm.

## Test before you promise

Keep some plots unchanged as a comparison. Without a control you cannot tell whether your advice or the weather made the difference.

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for agricultural and food scientists.
