## What the work actually looks like

You help the organisation decide not only whether it **can** use AI but whether it **should**, and how. That means reviewing projects, running impact assessments, advising on policy and law, training staff and keeping a clear record of decisions.

## Principles into practice

Most organisations already agree on principles like fairness, privacy and transparency. The hard part is applying them on a Tuesday afternoon to a real product. Good ethics officers build small, repeatable steps: a short intake form, a risk checklist, a named reviewer, and a clear path for escalation.
