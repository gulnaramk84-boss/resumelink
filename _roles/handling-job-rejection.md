## It is often not about you

Budgets change, internal candidates appear and priorities shift. You rarely see the full reason.

## Ask for feedback

```
Hello [name], thank you for letting me know. If you have a moment, what would have made my application stronger? I would be glad to be considered if a suitable role opens.
```

Not everyone replies.

## Find the weak stage

- No replies to applications: check your resume and role fit.
- Rejected after the first call: practise your introduction and discuss expectations earlier.
- Rejected after a task: ask someone skilled to review your work.

## Keep a simple tracker

Company, role, date, stage, next action. After a few weeks, patterns become visible.

## Protect yourself

Set hours for searching and hours without it. Talk with people. If low mood lasts or feels heavy, speak with a health professional.

## One step today

Improve one thing in your resume and send one carefully chosen application.
