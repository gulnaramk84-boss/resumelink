## What the work actually looks like

You assess, plan with the client, follow up, adjust and refer when needed.

## Check every AI plan

Allergies, conditions, medicines, energy needs, cost and whether the foods are available.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for dietitians and nutritionists.
