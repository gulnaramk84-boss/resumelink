## What the work actually looks like

You assess need and risk, choose and test technology, set storage and access rules, plan fallbacks and respond to incidents.

## Questions before deployment

Is it necessary? Is there consent or another legal basis? Where is data kept? Who is missed by the system? What is the other way in?

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for information security analysts.
