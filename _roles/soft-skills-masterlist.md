## Show, do not claim

```
[Situation] + [what you did] + [result]
```

## Skills and example proof

- **Communication:** explained a change to [group] and reduced repeat questions.
- **Teamwork:** helped deliver [project] with [N] people.
- **Problem solving:** found the cause of [problem] and fixed it.
- **Adaptability:** learned [tool] in [time] and used it for [task].
- **Leadership:** coordinated [people] without formal authority.
- **Time management:** met [N] deadlines while handling [load].
- **Empathy:** calmed an upset customer and agreed a solution.
- **Judgment:** noticed a risk and raised it early.

## How to choose

Read the advert, pick three skills it asks for and prepare one story for each.

## How to improve one

Choose one situation each week to practise, then ask a colleague what you did well and what to change.

## Avoid

Long adjective lists with no example.
