## Study the advert

Write down the three main tasks and match each with an example of yours.

## Five stories to prepare

A success, a mistake, a disagreement, an urgent task and learning something new.

```
Situation: where and what was happening.
Task: what you were responsible for.
Action: what you did.
Result: what changed and what you learned.
```

## Your one-minute introduction

```
I am a [role] with experience in [area]. I am strongest at [skill]; for example, [result]. I am interested in this role because [reason].
```

## Questions to ask them

- What does success look like after three months?
- Who would I work with most?
- What is the hardest part of this role now?
- What happens next?

## The day before

Check the time and place or link, print or open your resume, prepare clothes and sleep.

## After

```
Hello [name], thank you for your time. I especially enjoyed discussing [topic]. I am happy to send anything else you need.
```
