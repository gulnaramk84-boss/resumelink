## What the work actually looks like

You map skills, write and review materials, test them with learners and teachers and improve.

## Quality control

Keep a review checklist for accuracy, level, bias, accessibility and privacy.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for training and instructional occupations.
