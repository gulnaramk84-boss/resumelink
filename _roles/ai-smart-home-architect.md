## What the work actually looks like

You interview, design, specify, install or supervise, test, teach and maintain.

## Design for failure

Test what happens without internet, power or one vendor before handover.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for installers and computer network specialists.
