## What the work actually looks like

You design research workflows, check results, train colleagues and watch for tool errors.

## Verification protocol

Source, quotation, date, jurisdiction and current validity: check each before relying on it.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for lawyers and paralegals.
