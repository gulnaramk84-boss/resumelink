## What the work actually looks like

You observe, measure, simplify, test and agree team habits.

## Subtract first

List alerts, reports and steps nobody needs, and remove them before adding tools.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for similar titles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for industrial engineers and human factors occupations.
