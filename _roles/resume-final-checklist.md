## Top of the page

- Name, target role, phone, email and city.
- A professional email address.
- Two or three lines on who you are and what you offer.

## Experience

- Newest first.
- Each point shows an action and a result.
- Numbers only if they are true.

## A strong point

```
[What you did] + [how] + [what changed: number, time or scale]
```

## For screening software

- Standard headings: Experience, Education, Skills.
- A simple layout with no important text inside images.
- Words from the advert that honestly apply. Check with our [job comparison](/match-check.html).

## Before sending

- Read it aloud.
- Ask someone else to read it.
- Name the file Surname_Role.pdf.
- Remove personal data the employer does not need.
- Open the file on a phone.
