## What the work actually looks like

You watch the funnel, improve product pages and checkout, plan promotions, manage stock and support, and test ideas. AI helps with content and analysis, but you decide what is accurate and profitable.

## Calculate real margin

Include product cost, payment fees, shipping, returns, taxes and advertising before you judge a campaign.

## How to benchmark pay

Compare current postings for e-commerce manager or digital marketing roles in your market. For the United States, see the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) for marketing and sales managers.
